Welcome to Magenta

Open apps from the dock or search for them in Applications.
Files lets you create, open, rename, and delete files and folders on ext4.
Settings changes the desktop's appearance. Its Registry page controls
shortcuts, bars, and icon positions.

In the text interface, type a menu number and press Enter.
Type exit in the shell to return to Magenta.

Keep your writable disk when updating the boot ISO. That's where your
saved files live. Network settings last until the next restart.
