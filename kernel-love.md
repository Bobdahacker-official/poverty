# 💗 The Forbidden Love of Mr. Kernel, Mrs. Kernel, and Magenta 💗

It was a cold, lonely night. I was sitting at my computer, enjoying the beautiful presence of Magenta, the greatest operating system ever conceived by mankind.

Suddenly, I heard three knocks at my door.

**KNOCK. KNOCK. KNOCK.**

I slowly opened the door.

There he stood.

**Mr. Kernel.**

He was 7 feet tall, had 64-bit muscles, and smelled suspiciously like burnt thermal paste.

"YOU," he whispered romantically.

"Me?" I replied.

"YES. YOU HAVE BEEN USING MY KERNEL WITHOUT GIVING ME A GOODNIGHT KISS."

Before I could respond, Mr. Kernel grabbed me by the shoulders, looked deeply into my eyes, and said:

"Your filesystem is beautiful."

I blushed.

Then he ate me.

Not metaphorically.

**HE LITERALLY ATE ME.**

I was now inside Mr. Kernel's stomach, surrounded by corrupted memory pages, abandoned processes, and approximately 47 missing semicolons.

"HELP!" I screamed.

Mr. Kernel giggled.

"Segmentation fault, sweetheart."

I thought this was the end.

But then...

**BOOOOOOOM.**

The front door exploded into 8,192 tiny sectors.

Standing in the doorway was the most beautiful woman I had ever seen.

**MRS. KERNEL. 💋**

Her hair flowed like perfectly optimized assembly code. Her eyes sparkled like RGB fans running at maximum brightness. Her perfume smelled like fresh silicon and unconditional love.

She was holding a USB stick.

And on that USB stick...

**MAGENTA.**

"RELEASE MY BABY, YOU OVERCLOCKED MICROWAVE," she screamed.

Mr. Kernel trembled.

"B-but honey, I was just performing garbage collection!"

"YOU ATE THE DEVELOPER, GREGORY."

"My name isn't Gregory."

"IT IS NOW."

Mrs. Kernel inserted her Magenta USB stick directly into Mr. Kernel's forehead.

A beautiful pink light filled the room.

The heavens opened.

A choir of unemployed Linux developers began singing.

**MAGENTA HAS BOOTED.**

Mr. Kernel fell to his knees.

"NOOO! NOT THE BEAUTIFUL DESKTOP ENVIRONMENT! MY ONLY WEAKNESS!"

Mrs. Kernel opened the Magenta terminal and typed:

`sudo husband --stop-being-stupid`

Mr. Kernel immediately spat me out.

I landed on the carpet, covered in thermal paste and emotional damage.

Mrs. Kernel rushed toward me.

"Oh, my precious little executable," she whispered, gently stroking my forehead.

"Are you okay?"

"I think my bootloader is broken," I replied.

She smiled.

"Don't worry, darling. I'll always be your recovery partition."

My heart skipped a clock cycle.

She held my hand.

Our fingers intertwined like two perfectly compatible SATA connectors.

"Mrs. Kernel..." I whispered.

"Yes, my little ISO image?"

"Why did you save me?"

A single tear rolled down her cheek.

"Because, sweetheart..."

She leaned closer.

"...you are the only process I never want to terminate."

**MY CPU HIT 100% USAGE.**

Mr. Kernel watched from the corner, sobbing into his motherboard.

"BUT WHAT ABOUT OUR MARRIAGE?"

Mrs. Kernel turned around.

"OUR MARRIAGE HAS BEEN DEPRECATED SINCE VERSION 0.1."

Mr. Kernel screamed.

"YOU CAN'T JUST REPLACE ME WITH A MAN WHO WRITES C!"

Mrs. Kernel smiled.

"Watch me."

She turned back toward me and whispered:

"Would you like to mount my filesystem?"

I blushed so hard my GPU started thermal throttling.

"Only if it's read-write."

She gasped.

"How romantic."

We embraced beneath the soft glow of the Magenta desktop.

For the first time in my life, everything felt stable.

No kernel panics.

No corrupted files.

No mysterious triple faults.

Just love.

Pure, beautiful, 64-bit love.

And then Mr. Kernel stood up.

"WAIT A MINUTE."

We turned toward him.

He was holding a laptop.

"WHY DOES MAGENTA SAY IT'S A LINUX DISTRIBUTION? I'M NOT EVEN LINUX!"

Mrs. Kernel looked him dead in the eyes.

"Shut up, Gregory."

He shut up.

And we lived happily ever after.

Until the next morning, when Mrs. Kernel discovered that I had accidentally deleted her entire home directory.

She stared at me.

I stared at her.

Mr. Kernel slowly appeared behind us, holding a fork.

"Round two?"

**THE END. 💗**
